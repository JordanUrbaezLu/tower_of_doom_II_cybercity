Fleet and asteroids: fine surface detail

Irregular exposed alloy on raised panel lips, fine scuffs, restrained oxidation in recesses, weathered cargo enamel, heat-tempered machined nozzle collars, broken stone fractures and mineral inclusions. Color, normal, gloss and specular share the same surface masks. Hull shapes, nozzles, lights, movement and emission strengths are preserved.

Matched real Blender MCP renders use exported native maps and native gloss ranges with the shared approximate fullspec shader. Same cameras, lighting and exposure; bloom is disabled. These establish surface detail and coverage, not BO3 lighting, frame rate or native visual approval. The user performs the game test.
