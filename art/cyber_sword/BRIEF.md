# Cyber Sword

User direction: transform the existing Gold Battle Sword into a cyber weapon,
with neon blue/cyan, some purple, and a clearly illuminated fully-ready state.

Keep its broad blade silhouette, grip attachment, hand clearance and accepted
swing/slam animation tracks. Replace gold with dark blue-black alloy, satin
titanium cutting edges, rubber grip and restrained violet hardware. Preserve
the original in a separate reference file. Replace blade lettering with an
authored segmented power spine, fine cyan circuit paths and violet charge cells.
Rework the gem as a cyan reactor; keep narrow illuminated perimeter rails.

Charging: colored physical inlays, dark emission. Ready: cyan rails, reactor and
cells illuminate; dark metal stays dark. Active slam uses the same bright state.
Return to the dark state when charge is spent. Use the existing tier-three
Gravity Smash kill charge, including its current dev override; no independent
timer, new damage logic or animation replacement.

Preview at full length, close to the hilt, and in native grip space. Show matched
charging/ready renders and a transition preview. Bake actual fullspec maps and
explicit zero emissive depth. No added dynamic lights or particle fog. Native
appearance/performance remains a user test after a lead-cleared build.
