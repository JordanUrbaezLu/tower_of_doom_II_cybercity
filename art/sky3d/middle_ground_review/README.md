# Approved cyber middle ground

[Open the visual review](final_review.html). It compares the previous sky and the
new design at identical cameras. [The concept](concept.png) is the user-approved
art direction; [the original concept comparison](review.html) is preserved.

The sign uses engineered pylons, dimensional metal lettering and an angular
mechanical crest. The city pass modernizes 624 ordinary buildings and elevated
pods, with worn plate materials and controlled inset lights. The reduced layout,
station and all eight placed hero towers remain.

The final native build status and exact deployed bytes are recorded in
`build.json` from the completed guarded FULL build. `review_notes.json` records the
visual review stage; `composition_audit.json` and `architecture_check.json`
record geometry checks. These images are Blender projections, not game captures.

The title was authored with the built-in Imagegen tool. Its unchanged RGBA source,
exact prompt and provenance live in `../sign/v10_cyber_hero.*`. The concept prompt
is `prompt.txt`. The source image contains rendered metal relief; the Blender
card supplies placement, true-alpha coverage and calibrated sky radiance.

Run [open_preview.ps1](../../../tools/sky_middle_ground/open_preview.ps1) after
installation for the finished HDR panorama. Blender can reset shading to Solid
on file load; the launcher restores Rendered and Scene World/Scene Lights.
Numpad 0 returns to the title camera. The [reopened screenshot](blender_reopened.png)
and [activation receipt](preview_activation.json) verify the display against the
installed HDR. The launcher and activation helper were added after the build;
they affect preview display only and do not resave the snapshotted blend or sky.
The editable full city remains `../tod2_sky.blend`.

Native exposure, bloom and overall visual quality await the user's playtest.
No agent game launch. The case-safe asteroid fix remains intact.
