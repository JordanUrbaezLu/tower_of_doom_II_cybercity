HEADSHOT (id 6, A tier, 10 levels) is strictly dominated by DAMAGE (id 1, S tier, 10 levels) — 3x weaker per level AND conditional

Both cost 10 levels. DAMAGE gives +12%/Lv on EVERYTHING (+120% at cap). HEADSHOT gives +4%/Lv and only on a head hit (+40% at cap), and because it is ADDITIVE into the same mult sum it is +40% of BASE damage — not +40% of the already-tripled headshot.
