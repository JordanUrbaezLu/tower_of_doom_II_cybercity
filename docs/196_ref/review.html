The sky shown is session 22's new title sign after the −90° roll. Built in one combined full build (2026-09-29 11:49).

First play (13:00): the scene works, but in game the sign was not in the shot. The engine's own sky shader shows the image's centre faces east, not north, so the −90° roll put the sign behind the crown. The fix (session 22's new render at roll 0, plus the sky material turned 180°) puts it due west, exactly as these previews show. It rides the next full build (docs/196 §3).

The opening scene (each player's first jump)

timewhat happens
0.0 sLaunch. The letterbox closes, the HUD hides, the gun goes down, the wind rushes in. Dark smoke gathers over the sky sign (out of view).
0.7 sThe camera turns right, smoothly, to the sign (stock view-clamp lerp, never a snap).
1.5 sThe magenta gunship WARPS IN beside the dimmed sign: a portal flash, a deep bamf.
2.25 sThe green gunship warps in on the other side, bow to bow.
2.7 sEngines flare white-hot; they ram each other.
3.9 sTHE CRASH: the screen burns white, the fireball fills the frame, the shot shakes, the boom and its rumble.
4.1 sBehind the white, the sign ignites: a power surge, the title ablaze.
~5.5 sThe white clears through fire: TOWER OF DOOM II blazing, reactor blasts, wreckage falling to the city, 9c's fighter salute.
7.3 sThe swing back down to the tower; the blaze fades as the sign leaves the frame.
9.1 sControl returns: free look, bars open, HUD back, the gun comes up.
11.0 sA soft landing on the terrace.

Every later jump: 7 s with the same float and soft landing, free look throughout. A card deal mid-scene ends it at once (the ships and the effects go with it).
