## The audit

Every shipped cell was put beside the real gun's silhouette and asked one
question: *would a player who has held this gun in a Black Ops game name it
from the icon alone?* The per-cell answers are baked into
`current_set_audit.png` (generated by `tools/gen_gunhud_detail_ref.js`). The
short version:

| verdict | cells |
|---|---|
| **wrong family** — reads as a different kind of gun | **Magnum** (reads as a carbine, not a revolver), **Nail Gun** (a compressor), **Death Machine** (a box with lines, no barrel cluster), **RPG** (a tube with no rocket) |
| **generic** — right kind, but any gun of that kind | MAC-10, Krig 6, Bulldog, SG12, MOG 12, Enfield, HK21 vs Stoner, UDM vs MR6, RK7 |
| **good, add the tell** | MP5 (mag curve), MP7 (foregrip), SPAS-12 (heat-shield holes), AK-47 (gas tube), Stoner 63 (carry handle), Executioner (flutes), AMP63 (vents), knife (guard + serrations), wakizashi (tsuba), Stormbreaker (axe + hammer faces), riot shield (bars) |
| **keep** | Gift of Death (the detail ceiling), the two akimbo pairs (they follow their singles) |

The recurring cause is the same in every "generic" cell: the drawing has the
gun's OUTLINE but not its **one identifying feature** — the thing the real gun
is known for. Every cell in the brief names that one feature, and the ask is to
make it the biggest thing in the drawing.

## Why ONE sheet of 28 and not "fix these 12"

Line weight. The eight older cells (docs/95, v17.9) and the twenty newer ones
(docs/98, v17.40) were drawn in different passes and already differ slightly in
stroke; a third pass that touches half the set would leave three weights on one
HUD. Delivering all 28 as one image in one pass is the only way the set comes
back uniform, and the generator has now returned a stated-size fresh sheet
correctly twice (docs/95's 1728×264 and docs/98's 1440×528). The image NAMES do
not change — the 28 slugs are the ones the HUD already uses — so the install is
a slicer row swap and a full build, no Lua.

## What "more detail" means here

More of the gun's **SHAPE** — never thinner lines. The icon is shown at
144 × 66 pixels, a bit over half size, and anything narrower than 10 px on the
288 × 132 cell disappears there. So:

- add the identifying feature as a **big, simple shape** (a drum is a circle, a
  bipod is two bars, a scope is a cylinder on a block), never as fine linework;
- interior structure (magazine, grip, stock, bipod, wood, vents) goes in the
  **darker tone**, as hard-edged flat shapes;
- the outline stays ONE weight, about 7 px, all 28 cells;
- if a feature cannot be drawn 10 px wide, leave it out — a clean silhouette
  beats a smudge.

The Gift of Death cell on sheet A is the most detailed drawing in the set. That
is the ceiling. Nothing more detailed than that.

## The style — unchanged

- Flat vector art. **No** 3D, bevel, gloss, gradient, cast shadow or texture.
- Exactly **two flat tones**: steel-white face `#E8EEF6` and one cool grey-blue
  (sample it from the attached sheets) for the offset and interior structure.
- A crisp dark outline `#0A1020`, about **7 px**, around the whole silhouette.
- The **same offset direction** as every existing cell: down and to the right.
- No text, numbers, labels or grid lines anywhere in the image.
