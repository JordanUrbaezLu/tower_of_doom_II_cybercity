Power scale: map 1 at one tenth (damage and enemy health)

All damage and enemy health are at the map's power scale: map 1 at one tenth, so a hit reads 17 instead of 168 and time-to-kill is unchanged. DPS is single-target, body shots, no upgrades. Sustained = one full magazine plus a reload; burst = trigger held, no reload. Headshots are ×3 for every class. Pack-a-Punch is +25% damage, fire rate and magazine. Warrior figures are one target's damage per swing ÷ swing time. The gauntlet averages its full right hook and its ×0.75 left; both of the Cyber Sword's strokes count. A swing hits only its target until the CLEAVE card (2026-09-27 class pass 4): then up to 3 more (4 zombies at Lv9) in the blade's shape (punch: a 35° cone, full to 64 u, half to 100 u; gauntlet: within 128 u of the fist; sword: a ~53°-each-side arc to 120 u, both strokes). Pack-a-Punch doubles Warrior damage and makes the punch and gauntlet swing a quarter faster; Pack-a-Punch never changes the sword's 1.033 s swing (Arms Specialist does). Bow figures are HELD figures (2026-10-05, the automatic bow): hold the trigger and the bow fires itself at full draw (×3 per arrow) and draws again; a tap does ×1. Every hit on any enemy draws a hit marker (white hit, gold headshot, red punch on a kill).
