HEALING AURA heals more and, at its last level, revives a downed teammate. ARCHMAGE and BLINK deepen those two. MYSTICAL HANDS makes staff swaps three times faster.

==================================================
[h2]New[/h2]
[list]
[*]THE WARDEN KING. The Endless Spire now ends. Above the last hub hall is an open summit deck with cover pillars and a 1,000-point ammo crate. Once every living player is up there the stair seals, and ten seconds later he lands: one Panzer with 25,000,000 health per player, up to 100,000,000 for four. His flame is twice the size with a second cone thrown ahead of it, so the old safe distance is not safe. [h2]The map[/h2]
[list]
[*]A 50-floor open-air staircase spirals around a solid neon core: base arena at the bottom, rooftop arena with Pack-a-Punch and Mule Kick on top.
[*]Rounds never pause: the next round begins the moment the last zombie of the current one spawns.
[*]52 buyable doors, one per floor: 1125, rising 100 per floor, capped at 6000; roof door 7500; power room 750.
[*]Door prices scale with party size: x1.00 solo, x1.27 duo, x1.82 trio, x2.36 quad. Prompts show destination and price.
[*]Power switch at the bottom, at the far end of a long corridor outside the base's east wall.
[*]Breather lounges at floors 10, 20, 30 and 40: wide balconies with perk pads, Pack-a-Punch, a personal upgrade station and a teleporter.
[*]Breather Pack-a-Punch costs 5000, needs power, and packs your class gun.
[*]Each breather teleporter warps everyone on the pad back to the base: hold USE, 2.2 s charge, 60 s cooldown ("Teleporter recharging...").
[*]Tower gauge HUD: one lit cell per two floors climbed, amber breather cells, a lit roof crown and a red pip on the lowest live boss.
[*]Luck bar top-left; crosshair damage numbers (amber, bigger teal for headshots); the health readout shows your real maximum.
[*]The pause menu lists your owned upgrades: what each does at your level, how it triggers, and level pips against the cap.
[*]Powerups: Insta-Kill is a team-wide 3x damage window (bosses exempt); Time Warp slows regular zombies only; a free Pack-a-Punch drop once power is on.
[*]Guard rails can be hopped onto but never climbed over.
[*]After a wipe or the win, a menu offers Restart Map or End Game; no choice within 60 s returns to the lobby.
