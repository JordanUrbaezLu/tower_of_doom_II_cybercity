MAGE STAFF WEAPON ICONS — WHAT "CONSISTENT WITH THE GUN ICONS" MEANS, MEASURED

The three staff cells are in the game today. They were drawn in a pass of 25 upgrade CARDS, not beside the 28 weapon icons they actually sit with on the HUD, and it shows in three ways that can be counted rather than argued about. Every number below is measured off the shipped PNGs.

1 — THE SET YOU ARE JOINING  28 shipped weapon icons, true on-screen size (144 × 66)

One of these is on screen at all times, bottom-right, beside the health bar. Three flat tones, one heavy outline, no colour anywhere in the set.

Read that middle column again. Across all 28 shipped weapon icons, 0.2% of inked pixels are a saturated colour — i.e. none; what little there is comes from the blue-black outline itself. The three staffs are at 11.9–14.4% — roughly sixty times the set. The coloured gems are the single biggest thing separating these three cells from the set.

5 — SO HOW DOES A PLAYER TELL THEM APART? THE SAME WAY THEY TELL 28 GUNS APART.

By silhouette. The set already separates a combat knife, a wakizashi and a Stormbreaker with no colour at all. The player only ever holds one staff at a time, so this is recognition, not a side-by-side discrimination test — but the three heads should still be unmistakable at 144 × 66. Same shaft, same grip, same diagonal, same proportions on all three. Only the head changes, and it changes a lot.

The head subjects above are the ones already shipped and they are good — keep them. This pass is about the treatment: kill the gem colours, drop to three flat tones, thicken every stroke, and fill the cell the way the axe and the wakizashi do.
