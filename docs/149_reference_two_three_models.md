## Rig preservation and exports

Both validation reports hash every original donor mesh's vertices, faces and
skin weights. Four pose checks cover head, both arms and knees; worst error is
under 0.000016 source units. The first armored hand check caught unweighted
vertices generated by microscopic bevels. The final masters freeze evaluated
bevel geometry and bind every equipment vertex rigidly to its original joint.
This also avoids topology changing when live bevel modifiers see a moving pose.
Original donor geometry and weights were not changed.

Both atlases use five 4096-square maps and seven component-preserving equipment
LODs. Ref2 totals: 46,433 / 32,296 / 18,631 / 7,654 / 5,120 / 3,392 / 1,692.
Ref3 totals: 45,961 / 30,573 / 15,831 / 8,414 / 4,844 / 3,242 / 1,926. The armored stock body retains
its existing eight native generated LODs; its head has seven authored LODs.
Tiny collapsed equipment faces are cleaned before conversion. Reference two
uses a 0.002 minimum triangle altitude after a native converter discarded one
microscopic leg-equipment triangle; the original reference-one defaults stay
unchanged. Original donor triangles are never pruned. Compiled coverage allows
only the existing eight stock degenerates, not missing body parts.

`export_cyber_zombie_kit.py` now accepts both new scene types and exports isolated
derivatives under each study. `promote_exports.py` requires matching master and

The old regular Relay welder equipment is superseded. Signal's existing

## Armored reference correction

The user rejected the first backpack's generic cartridges and mirrored purple
hoses. `three_backpack_rebuild.py` replaces that assembly through live Blender
MCP: custom turned vessel profiles, short sight chambers, stepped upper caps,
heavy lower pressure housings, curved hazard panels, clamp ears, tie-rod lugs,
amber regulator pilots, fill ports, a sump manifold and central latch/warning
plate. One purple feed runs beside the pack. The opposite arch is a hinged metal
lifting bracket, not a second hose. The load frame has separate cushions,
uprights and crossmembers; the original armored vest remains intact beneath it.

`three_detail_correction.py` removes mirrored arm feeds and one duplicated head
loop. `three_visor_finish.py` replaces the oversized rectangular visor with a
compact clipped-corner rim, recessed smoked filter, tapered hood cheeks, a
continuous folded roof and brackets connected to the original rotary hinges.
The chin latch is lowered below the mouth. These scripts run after the initial
three authoring passes and before `fix_modifier_order.py` and `review.py`.
`three_clamp_fit.py` seats the arm saddles on the actual hose centerline;
independent donor-surface rays had placed one saddle on an original armor spike.

Reference two retains its own twin chest cells, louvered spinal unit and paired
limb braces. Reference three's reconstructed pressure pack is not used by the
regular zombie variants. The saved masters, baked derivatives and gallery must
all share the final validation hashes before delivery.
