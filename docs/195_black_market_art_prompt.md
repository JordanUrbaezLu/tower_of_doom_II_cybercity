# The Black Market: the dealer, the companion and the speed

## What this is for
In our cyberpunk zombies game, a masked black-market dealer runs a neon slot machine
(`installed_screen_1280x720.png`). The player pays, the reel spins, and it lands on an item for
their robot COMPANION. The screen works, but nothing on it is alive: nobody reacts to the spin, and
the player cannot see which of their robots is getting the item. This pack adds:

1. **THE DEALER**, in a small portrait window on the left, in 5 poses that change during the spin.
2. **THE COMPANION** getting the item, in the matching window on the right (4 robots).
3. **The portrait frame** both windows use.
4. **Speed streaks** laid over the spinning reel.

`screen_layout_new_pieces.png` shows exactly where each piece goes. Match the approved screen's style
exactly: flat, bold, graphic game-UI art; dark metal; hot magenta and cyan neon; the dealer's red.

## Hard rules
1. Exact sizes above, PNG, RGBA. Every size is a multiple of 4: keep it.
2. **No text anywhere** (the game writes the companion's name on the frame's plate itself).
3. **P1, the frame:** follow `portrait_frame_template_288.png` exactly. The PICTURE WINDOW
   (x 24-264, y 20-212) is **fully transparent**. The NAME PLATE (x 24-264, y 224-268) is an
   **empty dark recessed plate**. Everything else is the frame: dark metal bezel, a thin hot-magenta
   outer neon line and a thin cyan inner line (like `i_tod_spin_frame.png` and `i_tod_spin_socket.png`),
   four small screws. Transparent outside the frame's outline.
4. **D1-D5 and C1-C4, the portraits:** fully opaque, full bleed (a background fills the whole
   240 x 192), head-and-shoulders framing per `portrait_template_240x192.png`, nothing important
   within 10 px of the edges. All nine share ONE style, ONE camera distance and ONE lighting setup,
   so they read as a set.
5. **The dealer (D1-D5)** is the SAME character in all five, exactly as in `merchant.png`: a black
   hood and cloak, a black mask with RED neon X eyes and a jagged stitched RED neon grin, black
   gloves. His mask never changes shape; he acts with his head, hands and body, and the neon glow
   brightens with his mood. Background: his dark shop, red neon shelf lights, oxblood shadows
   (see `hero.png`). Each pose must read at 120 x 96 (the preview folders show that size).
6. **The companions (C1-C4)** keep the shapes in the clay renders (`companion_*.png`): the game
   models they must match. Background: a dark workshop wall lit cyan and magenta, the same for all four.
7. **S1, the streaks:** white and greys only, soft; at most about half the image covered, so the
   tiles still show through.
