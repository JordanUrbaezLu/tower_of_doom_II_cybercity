# TOWER OF DOOM II — HUD typeface, three options

All three share the current face's metrics exactly: letters 89 px total ink, bottom at y=103; numerals
137 px, bottom at y=151; face #E8EEF6, outline #0A1020, one hard shadow #62779C offset down-right
(5 px letters / 8 px numerals); face stroke ~16 px letters / ~25 px numerals; cell 28 empty.
Only the letterforms differ, so any option drops straight in.

## Option A — TOWER 1 REFINED (rev 2)
The current construction redrawn cleanly: one consistent small corner cut on every outer corner, one
stroke weight everywhere, tidier diagonals in K/V/W/X/Y, a wider apex on A, a squarer S and G, and the
1 given a proper flag and foot so it never reads as I. Rev 2 (legibility pass): every counter is larger
(bowl glyphs 3-6% wider, stroke trimmed to ~15/23 px, inner corner cuts almost gone) and the round
glyphs now carry real curves — O, C, G, Q, 0, 6, 8, 9 are rounded on all corners, while D, B, P, R keep
a flat, square-cornered stem and round only on the right, so D and O can no longer be confused.
Same widths and attitude as today — "the same font, drawn by a better hand".

## Option B — POPPINS MIX
Poppins-style geometry on the Tower 1 chassis: the bowls of O, C, G, Q, D, B, P, R, S, U, J and of
0, 2, 3, 5, 6, 8, 9 are true arcs (semicircles / stadium shapes), the 4 gets a diagonal arm and the
S is a two-arc spiral. Straight strokes stay flat-topped (E, F, H, I, L, T), the weight, outline and
hard shadow are untouched, so it stays a sibling — rounder and friendlier, not a different font.

## Option C — TOWER 1 SHARP
Everything pushed harder: 45-degree chamfers roughly twice the size of A's, small facet cuts inside the
counters, angled blade terminals on C, G, J, S, 2, 3, 5, a 6% narrower build, and two inner facets on
the 0 so it can never be mistaken for O. Still blocky, still bold, just more machined.

## Notes
- `_test.png` sets the section-6 lines at real on-screen scale (33% — the size of the two on-screen
  previews you sent). Mixed lines like AK-47 shrink the numerals to the letters' cap height so the
  two sheets read as one line; pure-numeral lines use the ammo-counter size and the wide numeral dash.
- Counters: every numeral counter is ≥ 30 px wide; the two bowls of the 8 (and 6/9 in B) are 19 px
  tall because two bowls plus a 25 px stroke do not fit 20 px each in a 112 px cap — the width is what
  reads, and it matches the current 8.
